# Model photos

One folder per model, named after its id in `src/data/products.ts` (for example `HG-MEZZO48/`). Every JPEG, PNG,
WebP or AVIF in it shows on the model's page as a gallery, in file-name order: the first one is also the photo on its
cards and in its structured data. Give each photo alt text (and a caption or credit if it needs one) in
`src/data/productPhotos.ts`.

Only photos HMI may publish: its own showroom and install photos, images from a manufacturer's dealer media kit, or
openly licensed photos of that exact model (CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA — never NC or ND), credited under the photo with
links to the source and the license. Never copy or hotlink images from a manufacturer's website.
